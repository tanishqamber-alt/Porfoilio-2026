Hi, I’m Tanishq.

I grew up in Gaya, Bihar, mostly on the internet. I spent most of my time on Google, hunting down websites and wondering why some looked great and felt effortless while others just didn’t. That curiosity never left.

Following it down the rabbit hole, I ended up at NIFT. Six years on, I design the kind of products I grew up exploring, and I still study what makes them work so well.

My expertise is fintech and wealth products. I make complex money decisions feel simple, clear and trustworthy. Lately I’ve been working on AI-led experiences and lending journeys.

I see the things most people never notice, and I make products so intuitive and natural they don’t need explaining. They just reveal themselves. Good design should feel obvious once it exists.

Off-screen I stun people with card tricks, make art or cook. Ask for one when we meet.

My inbox is open for design roles and good conversations. Say hello.

How I work

Many ideas, one clear version.

I explore widely, then merge the best of it into one structured design, and keep improving after launch. Morty helps.

  1. Listen

    Talk to users and stakeholders on site. Find the workaround they rely on.

  2. Frame

    Map every path and agree on the one problem worth solving.

  3. Branch out

    Sketch several directions on paper, quickly, before anything goes into Figma.

  4. Review

    Test with users, debate with evidence, drop what doesn’t hold up.

  5. Merge & refine

    One structured version ships, then keeps improving with real usage.

What I can do for you.

Six years across fintech, B2B SaaS and consumer products, from first problem statement to shipped product.

  • End-to-end product design

    From framing the problem with product and engineering to flows, interaction and final UI, and staying with it through launch.

  • Zero to one

    Turning early, messy ideas into a clear first version, and knowing what to leave out.

  • Design systems

    Components, patterns and design QA that help teams ship faster and keep every screen consistent.

  • Dashboards & data-dense UI

    Structuring complex information so people can understand it quickly and act on it.

  • Research & experimentation

    Usability tests, A/B tests and analytics that guide decisions, then iterating on what the data shows.

  • AI product design

    AI experiences people can trust: clear about what the system is doing, easy to check, and easy to correct.

Outside of work, a little window into my world.

My other studio.

I love cooking. That’s my studio. Thinking about systems and processes tires my brain, in a good way, but still. That’s when cooking comes in. It feels meditative, and you won’t believe it, but that’s where I get most of my ideas.

When I dug into why, I found that when your brain is focused on a routine task, other parts of it wander off and explore. I think that’s what cooking does for me. And of course, it feels great too. My speciality is Bihari Kadhai Chicken. I’ll get you to try it.

My side quest.

The first card trick I ever saw was on TV, as a kid. I still remember that feeling vividly: I was stunned. That was the day I decided I had to learn it. I wanted to be the guy doing magic, stunning people and watching their faces.

The first time I got internet, I went on YouTube and searched “how to do card tricks”. Today, after all these years, I am that guy. The one who watches someone’s face when it lands, and feels that high.

The dreamA live show for a crowd. Coming soon.
